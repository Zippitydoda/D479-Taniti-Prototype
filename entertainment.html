Sightseeing & Entertainment

Adventure awaits at every corner.

Top Sightseeing

Most people visit Taniti to enjoy the beaches, explore the rainforest, and to visit the volcano. Other popular activities include boat or bus tours of the island.

Activities & Nightlife

There are plenty of other things to do during your stay:

  • Visiting a local history museum.
  • Going on chartered fishing tours or snorkeling.
  • Zip-lining in the rainforest.
  • Taking helicopter rides.
  • Playing at an arcade, visiting art galleries, and bowling.
  • Visiting several pubs, including a microbrewery.
  • Dancing at a new dance club or seeing a movie.

Coming Soon

A new nine-hole golf course is currently under development and should be operational by next year! (Note: Many of the island's activities, including this upcoming course, are located in Merriton Landing.)
