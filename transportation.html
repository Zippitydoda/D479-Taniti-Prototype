Navigating our tropical island.

Arriving in Taniti

Almost all visitors arrive to Taniti by air, though some arrive on a small cruise ship that docks in Yellow Leaf Bay for one night per week. Taniti is served by a small airport that can accommodate small jets and propeller planes. Taniti is in the process of expanding the airport so larger jets will be able to land on the island within the next few years.

Ground Transportation

Getting around the island is easy with these options:

Transport Mode Service Area Hours / Details
Public Buses Taniti City 5:00 a.m. to 11:00 p.m. every day
Private Buses Rest of the island Schedules vary
Taxis & Rentals Taniti City / Airport Rental cars available near the airport
Bicycles Island-wide Available from several vendors (helmets required by law)

Taniti City is fairly flat and very walkable. The popular Merriton Landing area is also easy to explore on foot.
