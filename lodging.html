Places to Stay

Rest and relax in paradise.

Accommodation Options

Taniti has a wide variety of lodging that ranges from an inexpensive hostel to one large, four-star resort.

For visitors looking for a more personal touch, there are many small, family-owned hotels and a growing number of bed and breakfasts scattered around the island.

Popular Area: Merriton Landing

Many tourists choose to stay in the area surrounding Merriton Landing, which is a rapidly developing area on the north side of Yellow Leaf Bay.

Quality & Safety

Guests can rest easy knowing that all types of lodging are strictly regulated and regularly inspected by the Tanitian government.
